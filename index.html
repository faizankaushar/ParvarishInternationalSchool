Our K-8 Programs

Every stage of learning is designed to spark curiosity and confidence.

Playgroup

(2-3 Years)

  • Hands-on early learning with play-based activities
  • Developing language and motor skills
  • Encouraging curiosity and social confidence
  • Age-appropriate exploration through games and stories

Nursery

(3-4 Years)

  • Building essential life and communication skills

(Grade 4-8)

  • Growing academic skills in science, math, and language

  • Preparing students for higher classes and competitive exams
  • Encouraging leadership and collaboration

Anglo-Arabic Language Program

Our Anglo-Arabic language path introduces students to bilingual fluency with engaging lessons, spoken practice, and cultural connection.

Students from Grade 1 onward receive structured English language support, and from Grade 4 they begin guided Arabic language exposure. The program is built around conversation, reading, and pronunciation confidence.

  • English reading, writing, and speaking across subjects
  • Introductory Arabic vocabulary, script familiarity, and recitation practice
  • Storytelling, poetry, and role play that make both languages fun
