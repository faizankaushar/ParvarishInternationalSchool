Our K-8 Programs

Every stage of learning is designed to spark curiosity and confidence.

Playgroup

(2-3 Years)

  • Hands-on early learning with play-based activities
  • Developing language and motor skills
  • Encouraging curiosity and social confidence
  • Age-appropriate exploration through games and stories

Nursery

(3-4 Years)

  • Building essential life and communication skills
  • Learning through art, music, and guided discovery
  • Cultivating creativity, curiosity, and independence
  • Preparing children for formal class learning

Primary Years

(Grade 1-3)

  • Foundational literacy and numeracy development
  • Project-based learning and critical thinking
  • Healthy social interaction and confidence building

Middle School

(Grade 4-8)

  • Growing academic skills in science, math, and language
  • Developing problem-solving, creativity, and study habits
  • Preparing students for higher classes and competitive exams
  • Encouraging leadership and collaboration

Proximity and Location

Located in the heart of Hamzapur, Sherghati, our school offers easy access to local markets, community centers, and major transport routes. It’s the ideal choice for families seeking a trusted K-8 education nearby.

Specialized Curriculum

We go beyond traditional learning with Heureka—a visible thinking curriculum inspired by Harvard’s Project Zero. This unique approach sparks curiosity, nurtures critical thinking, and encourages imagination in young minds.

Features

Safety is of great importance for us. We provide a secure campus, trained staff, CCTV surveillance, and child-friendly infrastructure to ensure your child is safe all the time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about admissions, curriculum, and our K-8 experience.

We enroll students from Playgroup through Grade 8 across our full K-8 program.

How can I apply for admission?

Submit your details through the admissions form on this page or contact the school directly via phone or email to schedule a campus visit.

What curriculum does the school follow?

We follow a play-based and discovery-driven K-8 curriculum designed to build language, social, cognitive, and creative skills.
